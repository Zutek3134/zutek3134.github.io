Collectibles

Cubes

There are 10 per level.

Spheres

There are 0 to 3 per level. They are merely surprises for the players to discover, as they often lead to secret hidden paths, or require special combination of taps.

Lights

Players can activate lights for each level. These are a simple way of knowing the progress for a level.

Blue Light

Reach at least 50% of the level.

Orange Light

Complete the level and collect all cubes without enabling guidance lines. Also known as a perfect run.

Purple Light

The conditions of the Orange Light, plus collecting all spheres, if any. Also known as a spectacular run.

Guidance Lines

Shows the path to pass the level. To enable this feature in a certain level, players must reach the 1st checkpoint of that level first.

2D Mode

Turns the camera angle into a top-down angle and switches view from perspective to orthographic. Note that levels were not made with this feature in mind, so there will be unexpected behaviours. To enable this feature in a certain level, players must at least have the orange light lit up for that level.

Roadmap

For me, this game demands ample time and extreme focus to develop, which unfortunately I currently lack. I have turned to simpler and easier projects at the moment. It is unknown when I will be able to revisit this work.

New Anniversary Level

Scheduled to release May 2023, but never released due to limited time available at that time.

UI/UX Rework

My coding experience with Unity remained amateur at that time, and the growing code base became more and more difficult to untangle to the point opening the project required mental preperation. When I do come back to the game, I will have to do a major overhaul.
